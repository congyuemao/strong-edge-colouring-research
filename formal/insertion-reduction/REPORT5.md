**The triangle example following Lemma 11.20 repeats a false assertion.** Its claim that both outer sets are the whole set `Z`, and that `Miss(uw)` contains all colours on `Y`, is not valid without an additional local exclusion. The counterexample already recorded in `REPORT.md` applies here too: `e=01`, `uw=02`, and the edge `57` belongs to `Z` and `Y`, but conflicts with both `01` and `02`. Therefore it is not in the outer set `O(02)`, which by definition excludes `N(01)`. If the twenty coloured edges have distinct colours, its colour is not in `Miss(02)` either. The existing kernel proofs are `AuditExamples.example_overlap_conflicts_e`, `example_overlap_conflicts_uw`, and `example_overlap_in_Z` in [AuditExamples.lean](Se20Insertion/AuditExamples.lean). This diagnoses the example, not a counterexample to Lemma 11.20 or to triangle insertion. The new `outer_cover`, `single_outer_cover`, and `single_outer_union_bound` prove the abstract covering statements with the actual outer sets. They make no disjointness assumption about `Z`.

**The single-edge gain formula needs a distinct target colour, `b ≠ p`.** If “recolour” already means changing to a different colour, this is an implicit hypothesis that must be made explicit in Lean. Otherwise the written hypotheses admit a counterexample. Take the path on six vertices with successive edges `e0,...,e4`; colour only `e0,e2,e4`, with colours `x,p,x`, where `x ≠ p`, and let `f=e2`, `b=p`. This is a proper partial strong colouring in the ambient path. Colour `p` is free at `f`, and recolouring to `p` changes nothing. For colour `x`, however, deleting `f` leaves its two neighbours in two components, so `k_x=2`, whereas the original `(b,x)` graph is a connected three-vertex path, so `j_x=1`. All other summands vanish. The claimed gain is 1 while the actual gain is 0. This small counterexample is a mathematical description, not a new Lean certificate. The corrected general formula with `b ≠ p` remains among the unfinished items below.

**The boundary gap in the supplied proof of Corollary 11.8 can be repaired.** As reported in the first pass, at chain length 19 there is only one unused colour, so the proof cannot choose two unused colours at the last vertex. The repair uses the preceding prefix: if the final vertex lacks colour `c`, its predecessor has a trivial separable pair consisting of `c` and the last moved colour. Both colours are outside the prefix's colour set. This contradicts failure of Theorem 11.7 on all shorter chains. `chain_obstruction_saturated` proves this argument without a length-18 restriction. The general chain insertion theorem, nonseparability, extension, and numerical count are now checked. This is a repair of the proof, not a weakening of the corollary.
